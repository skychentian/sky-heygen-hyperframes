# Known Failure Patterns

- **Wrong ratio:** use 9:16/16:9 for video masters and 3:4/4:3 for the two cover entry points.
- **Duplicate credit spend:** do not submit the same HeyGen script a second time just to inspect a layout issue; fix HyperFrames or the cover layer instead.
- **Heavy cover:** a large dark rectangle, close-up face, or title covered by the person breaks the established Sky visual system.
- **Weak hook:** an opening such as “今天跟大家聊一下” is not a golden 3-second hook; find the complete viewpoint first.
- **Premature packaging:** do not use transitions, BGM, or motion graphics to hide an incomplete or awkward HeyGen take.
- **False completion:** queued/rendering, browser-open, or file-created is not the same as completed and quality-checked.
